Using Google Calendar?

Google can't use the Subscribe button. Open Google Calendar on a computer, then Other calendars → + → From URL and paste the https:// link from the grey button above. Google refreshes external calendars on its own schedule, usually every 8 to 24 hours.

All times are Europe/Rotterdam (CET in winter, CEST in summer). Feeds are rebuilt twice a day. Fixtures with an unconfirmed kickoff appear as all-day events marked “kickoff time TBD”. Cup rounds appear once the draw has been made, so the KNVB Beker may be missing early in the season.

Football data provided by the Football-Data.org API. KNVB Beker fixtures via ESPN.
