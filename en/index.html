Every Feyenoord fixture in your own calendar

Set it up once. After that your calendar keeps itself up to date, even when a match is moved.

  1. Choose how you want to see it

    You get two calendars, Home and Away, each in its own colour.

    You get one calendar with every match. Don't add Home or Away as well, or every match shows up twice.

  2. Add it to your calendar

    Subscribe to Home Subscribe to Away
    Subscribe to All

    Your calendar asks whether to subscribe. Tap Subscribe.

    1. Copy the link below.
    2. Open calendar.google.com on a computer. The phone app can't do this.
    3. Choose Other calendars → + → From URL and paste the link.
    Homehttps://legioenagenda.nl/feyenoord-home.ics Awayhttps://legioenagenda.nl/feyenoord-away.ics
    Allhttps://legioenagenda.nl/feyenoord-all.ics

    Google refreshes every 8 to 24 hours. After that it shows up on your Android phone too.

    1. Copy the link below.
    2. Choose Add calendar → Subscribe from web.
    3. Paste the link, give it a name and click Import.
    Homehttps://legioenagenda.nl/feyenoord-home.ics Awayhttps://legioenagenda.nl/feyenoord-away.ics
    Allhttps://legioenagenda.nl/feyenoord-all.ics

  3. Give each calendar a colour

    Open the list of calendars in your calendar app and pick, say, red for Home and grey for Away. You never have to do anything again.

    Done

    You never have to do anything again. New and rescheduled matches appear by themselves.

Moved? It moves too.

A rescheduled match moves in your calendar, without ever creating a duplicate. If the kickoff time isn't set yet, you see an all-day event.

Rotterdam time

Summer and winter time are handled for you, and kickoff times stay right when you are abroad.

Every competition

Eredivisie and Champions League, plus the KNVB Beker once each round is drawn. Updated twice a day.

Football data provided by the Football-Data.org API. KNVB Beker fixtures via ESPN.
